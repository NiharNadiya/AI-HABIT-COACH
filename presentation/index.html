Problem Statement & Clinical Motivation

The Sedentary Ergonomics Crisis

02 / 12

⚠️ Biomechanics of "Text Neck" Syndrome

At neutral posture (0°), the human head imposes 10–12 lbs of gravitational load on the cervical spine.

0° Flexion (Neutral): 10–12 lbs (4.5–5.4 kg)
15° Tilt: 27 lbs (12.2 kg)
30° Tilt: 40 lbs (18.1 kg)
60° Slouch (Typical): 60 lbs (27.2 kg) on C1–C7 vertebrae!

Continuous 60-lb shear torque causes chronic disc herniation, cervicogenic headaches, and permanent spinal remodeling.

📏 International Clinical Grounding

ISO 11226: Ergonomics — Evaluation of static working postures.
RULA Standard: Rapid Upper Limb Assessment for postural fatigue.
WHO Guidelines: Stratified daily hydration & 30-min break limits.

❌ Critical Flaws in Existing Solutions

  • Static Pomodoro Timers: Blind to human physiological state. They alert when you are in flow, and stay silent when you slouch.
  • Wearable Posture Sensors: High initial cost ($150–$300), battery recharging friction, and over 72% abandonment rate within 3 weeks.
  • Notification Fatigue: Generic popups ("Sit straight!") induce cognitive blindness; users dismiss them without behavioral adoption.
  • Cloud Privacy Concerns: Traditional camera-based systems stream raw video to external servers, violating workplace privacy laws (GDPR/HIPAA).
Key Insight: The goal is an ambient, zero-cost, privacy-preserving, and adaptive intervention.

Computer Vision & Kinematics

Edge Landmark Geometry & Filtering

04 / 12

📍 Key Anatomical Landmarks Tracked

From MediaPipe's 33 3D keypoints, we isolate upper-body postural landmarks:

  • Tragus of Ears: Landmarks 7 (Left) and 8 (Right)
  • Acromion of Shoulders: Landmarks 11 (Left) and 12 (Right)
  • Greater Trochanter of Hips: Landmarks 23 (Left) and 24 (Right)
  • Nose Reference: Landmark 0 for forward focal depth

〰️ Jitter Elimination (EMA Smoothing)

x̂_t = α · x_t + (1 - α) · x̂_{t-1}, where α = 0.35

Removes sensor noise, hand shadows, and micro-movements without introducing perceptible latency lag.

📐 Kinematic Formulations

1. Craniovertebral Vector Angle:

θ_neck = atan2(y_ear - y_shoulder, x_ear - x_shoulder) × (180° / π)

2. Torso Verticality:

θ_torso = atan2(y_shoulder - y_hip, x_shoulder - x_hip) × (180° / π)

3. Shoulder Asymmetry / Lateral Tilt:

Tilt_shoulder = (|y_11 - y_12| / ||v_11 - v_12||) × 100%

Indicates lateral spinal listing, un-ergonomic mouse elevation, or uneven armrest resting.

Executed entirely in client WebAssembly at 30 FPS.

1-Click Anthropometric Calibration

05 / 12

📐 The "Laptop Pitch Angle" Distortion

Why naive 2D posture algorithms fail in commercial deployment:

  • Laptop placed on a low desk looks upward at the user's chin at an angle of +15° to +30°.
  • An external monitor placed high looks downward.
  • A fixed absolute threshold (e.g. neckAngle > 60°) results in a massive false-positive slouch rate on laptops.

⏱️ 5-Minute Sliding Window Accumulator

Slouches are only confirmed if sustained across sliding 5-min intervals (1→5, 2→6, 3→7 min), preventing alerts when taking a quick sip of coffee or stretching.

🎯 The Solution: Personalized Relative Baseline

Step 1: 3-Second Median Sampling Buffer

θ_baseline = median({ θ_1, θ_2, ..., θ_90 })

Step 2: Relative Angular Deviation (Δθ)

Δθ = |θ_current - θ_baseline|

Step 3: Clinical ISO 11226 Decision Boundary

Posture State = Upright (Good) if Δθ ≤ 14.0°
Posture State = Slouch (Bad) if Δθ > 14.0°

✓ Completely camera-invariant, chair-invariant, and height-invariant.

Persisted in browser localStorage — eliminates calibration friction on subsequent sessions.

Machine Learning • Model 2

Contextual Bandit for Habit Formation

07 / 12

🧠 Solving Notification Fatigue

Traditional wellness apps fail because repeating "Drink Water" creates sensory desensitization. We model intervention as an online Reinforcement Learning problem.

🎯 5 Clinically Validated Action Arms (A)

  • neck_stretch: Isometric cervical retractions
  • hydration_sip: 150–250ml targeted water sip
  • standing_walk: 60–90 second active movement
  • eye_rest_20: 20-20-20 rule for digital eye strain
  • shoulder_retraction: Scapular pinches for thoracic kyphosis

⚖️ UCB1 (Upper Confidence Bound) Policy

Score_i = μ̂_i + c · √( (2 · ln N) / n_i )

Exploitation term (μ̂_i): Recommends arms the user has reliably completed in the past.

Exploration term (√(2 ln N / n_i)): Boosts un-tested arms so the coach continuously discovers new habit receptivity.

✓ Closed-Loop Reward: When user clicks "+1 Reward" in the UI, state updates instantly: n_i ← n_i + 1, μ̂_i ← μ̂_i + (r - μ̂_i)/n_i.
Provable sublinear regret: Adapts uniquely to each individual user within 15–20 interactions.
